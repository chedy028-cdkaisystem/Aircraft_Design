# X1 Design Study

Independent study of the Heart Aerospace X1. All numbers are estimates from AVL, OpenVSP and OpenFOAM.

- video_brief.md: brief for the YouTube video
- video/: the explainer video, its captions and narration timing, and the page it is rendered from
- battery/: battery and mission energy, structural wing pack
- results/: AVL scheme comparisons (A, B / B-opt, C canard)
- cfd/cfd_summary.md: OpenFOAM CFD results
- report_*/: CFD charts
